# Audit 2026-09-29 — Cross-Document Consistency Pass

**Scope.** A line-by-line read of the 14 papers, 15 scripts and 18 results files
in `papers/`, plus `index.html`, `README.md`, `ROADMAP.md` and `sitemap.xml`. The
purpose was to find and resolve conflicts between documents, and to trace every
reported figure back to the script and line that produces it.

**Outcome.** Six citation and attribution errors, three arithmetic errors, one
dimensional mislabel, one file corruption, three internal contradictions, and one
substantive physics error of which more below. All are recorded below whether or
not they were fixed. The earlier session record
(`SESSION-2026-09-28-PROVENANCE-VERIFICATION.md`) remains authoritative for
withdrawals predating this pass.

## 1. Withdrawn: the MICROSCOPE bound on the coupling

**The most consequential finding of this audit.** The black hole paper derived a
bound on the gravity-sector coupling `c_g` from MICROSCOPE, and used it to
predict a freeze-out standoff of `2.5e-5 r_s` outside the Schwarzschild radius.
Both are withdrawn.

MICROSCOPE compares two test masses — titanium and platinum-rhodium — in the same
field, in the same orbit, 21 cm apart. It bounds the *fractional difference* in
the gravitational constant between two materials. The framework's coupling is
written `G_eff = G_0 (1 - c_g G)`, and if `c_g G` is universal across materials it
enters both test masses identically and cancels in the ratio:

```
a_Ti / a_Pt = (m_Ti / m_Pt) · (1 + c_g G_Ti) / (1 + c_g G_Pt)
                                     ── G_Ti = G_Pt ──>  m_Ti / m_Pt
```

So a universal coupling is **common mode** and MICROSCOPE returns a null whatever
its value. What MICROSCOPE does bound is the *composition-dependent* part of the
coupling — and `buoyancy-origin.html` §3.2 establishes that part as exactly zero,
because the time field is a property of the source mass and carries no information
about the responding body. The framework therefore predicts the MICROSCOPE null by
construction, and MICROSCOPE constrains `c_g` not at all.

The same cancellation removes the chronometric-levelling bound. A frequency
difference between two clocks at geopotentials `U1, U2` receives the coupling only
as a factor-of-two correction to the potential difference it already measures:

```
Δν/ν = Δ(Φ/c²) + 2 c_g G_bg Δ(Φ/c²)
```

The Paris–PTB residual of `(4.7 ± 5.0)e-17` on a potential difference of
`4.36e-14` yields `c_g G_bg < 5.7e-4`, which is no bound worth having.

**What survives.** The closed-form freeze-out result is untouched:

```
r_freeze / r_s = (1 + sqrt(1 + c_g²)) / 2   >  1   for all c_g > 0
```

That is the framework's quantitative claim and it needs no value of `c_g`. What is
withdrawn is any *specific* standoff. The corpus previously cited
`c_g < 10⁻²` from a 2012 MICROSCOPE limit, and separately a 2022 final result at
`2.7e-15` in a different paper; neither was a bound on this quantity.

**Why this is a stronger position, not a weaker one.** An unconstrained `c_g`
means the standoff is not pinned near the horizon. The framework's claim is
qualitative and parameter-free — no horizon forms, no singularity — and it now says
so plainly instead of attaching a number that was resting on a cancellation.

## 2. Corrected: the remnant-density derivation

The paper states `rho_rem = 2 V(phi)/c^2` and the script evaluated `2*V0` with the
`1/c^2` factor dropped. **The paper's derivation is self-consistent and the script
was the erroneous one.** Balancing the residual gravitational pull against the
field's repulsion and substituting `M = (4/3) pi r^3 rho_rem`:

```
GM/r^2   = (8 pi G / 3) r V/c^2
(4/3) pi G r rho  = (8 pi / 3) G r V / c^2
rho     = 2 V / c^2                      1/c^2 present, correct
```

The script's `2*V0` gave `1.40 rho_crit`, which is `2 * 0.7` with `c^2` treated as
unity. Corrected: `rho_crit = 8.53e-27 kg/m^3`, `V0 = 5.97e-27`, and

```
rho_rem = 2 V0 / c^2 = 1.33e-43 kg/m^3 = 1.56e-17 rho_crit
```

Seventeen orders of magnitude below cosmic density rather than at it. The remnant
is diffuse, not compact, which is the conclusion the paper reached from the wrong
number. Its size follows from the bound mass fraction `f`:
`r = (3 f M / (4 pi rho_rem))^(1/3)`, giving 1.6e8 ly for `f = 0.1` down to 348 ly
for `f = 1e-18` on a 10 Msun collapse. No value of `f` is asserted, since no
remnant mass or abundance is asserted anywhere in the corpus.

The script now evaluates `2*V0/c**2`, the check passes, and the count is 9 of 9.

## 7. Corrected: internal contradictions

**Entanglement, §5.4 against §4.2 and §6.3.** §5.4 asserted that `dτ_A/dt =
dτ_B/dt` is *required* for entanglement, which contradicts §4.2 and §6.3, both of
which state that preservation holds even when the two differ. Resolved: the
equality is the condition for the *accumulated* phase difference to be stationary,
which is not the entanglement invariant. Both statements now kept distinct.

**Decoherence, two incompatible scalings.** §4.4/§5.5 give
`t_dec ∝ 1/|sin(δ/2)|`; §6.4 gives `t_dec ∝ (6ε/|d²(δG)/dt²|)^{1/3}`. These are
distinct regimes rather than competing estimates — phase offset versus curvature —
and are now labelled as such in the closure table.

## 8. Resolved: the two definitions of G

The corpus used one symbol, `G`, for two different quantities: the background
amplitude `G(t) = 10⁻³ + 10⁻⁴cos(2πt)` carried by the correction law, and the local
radial profile `d(dτ/dt)/d(ln r)` used in the collapse analysis. Writing both as `G`
is what made them look like competing definitions.

They are one field in two roles. The link is already in the corpus — the radial
profile returns the background amplitude in the far field, `𝒢(x) → 1/(2x) → 0` as
`x → ∞`, which is a field *sourced* by the mass and vanishing in deep space rather
than a constant offset. The local profile is now written `𝒢(x)` throughout
`blackhole-lifecycle.html` and `theory-of-everything.html`, with the relationship
stated explicitly.

## 11. Open: local-observable construction of the field

The freeze-out mechanism runs on `𝒢(x) = d(dτ/dt)/d(ln r)` diverging as `r → r_s`.
That divergence is a property of the Schwarzschild *solution* — of the coordinate
system chosen — and not an independent fact about nature; an infalling observer
sees nothing special at the horizon. The framework has not shown that `𝒢` is a real
field with physical divergence rather than bookkeeping.

This is a derivation task, not a measurement task, and it needs no data beyond what
the corpus already transcribes. `chronometric-levelling.html` §1 establishes that
two clocks at different geopotentials measure `dτ/dt` directly as a frequency ratio
with no mass model. Building `𝒢` from that reading, rather than from a global
solution, is what would make the divergence a claim about clock rates converging
somewhere physically real. Until then the singularity result rests on a
coordinate-dependent quantity.
