\[F \equiv -\left\langle \frac{\partial^2 \ln \mathcal{L}}{\partial G^2} \right\rangle = \sum_{i=1}^N \frac{Y_{0,i}^2 c_i^2}{\sigma_i^2}\]

\[\sigma_G^2 \ge F^{-1} = \left( \sum_{i=1}^N \frac{Y_{0,i}^2 c_i^2}{\sigma_i^2} \right)^{-1}\]
