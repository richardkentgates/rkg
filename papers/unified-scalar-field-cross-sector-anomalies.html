1. Abstract

A wide range of physical anomalies—including nuclear decay-rate deviations, lunar gravitational MASCON residuals, inertial navigation discrepancies, and cosmological scalar-field ratios—cluster in the same fractional deviation band of \(10^{-4}\) to \(10^{-3}\). This paper presents a unified scalar field \(G(t)\) that explains these anomalies through a single correction law \(X_{\mathrm{eff}} = X_0(1 + c_X G)\). We demonstrate forward–backward consistency: given any \(G(t)\), the theory predicts sector deviations; given sector deviations, the same \(G(t)\) is reconstructed. This establishes the scalar field as a measurable physical quantity with cross-domain coherence.

The stress–energy tensor is:

\[T_{\mu\nu}^{(G)} = \nabla_\mu G\nabla_\nu G - g_{\mu\nu}\left( \frac{1}{2}(\nabla G)^2 + \frac{1}{2} m_G^2 G^2 \right).\]

\[w = \frac{p_G}{\rho_G}.\]
